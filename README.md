# DISK — Ricochet Throwing Weapon & Shield Mod

**DISK** adds a modular, customizable throwing chakram and shield weapon to **Mineclonia** and **Minetest Game**[cite: 1, 2]. Bank trick shots off walls to gain bonus damage[cite: 1, 2], slice through lines of enemies[cite: 1, 2], shatter fragile blocks[cite: 1, 2], trigger buttons/targets from afar[cite: 1, 2], and customize your weapon's flight physics and special abilities at an Anvil or Steel Block[cite: 1, 2].

---

## Controls & Combat Mechanics

* **Throw Disk (`Left-Click`):** Launches your Disk[cite: 1, 2]. It flies through the air with realistic momentum decay, bouncing off solid walls and slicing through enemies[cite: 1, 2]. When its momentum runs out, it embeds itself into the surface—walk over it to pick it back up!
* **Manual Tech Recall (`Right-Click`):** If your Disk is forged with the **Tech Core**, pressing Right-Click at any time (even with an empty hand while the Disk is stuck in a wall or flying) activates the electromagnet recall, pulling it straight back into your hand.
* **Shield Bash & Projectile Parry (`Sneak + Left-Click`):** When using the **Round Shield** outer shell, Sneak + Left-Clicking unleashes a close-range shockwave that knocks back nearby enemies and deflects incoming arrows, fireballs, and snowballs.
* **Open Blueprint Forge (`Sneak + Right-Click` on Anvil / Steel Block):** Opens the **DISK Blueprint Forge** GUI where you can swap Outer Shells, Core Modules, Structural Dyes, and repair durability[cite: 1, 2].
* **Momentum Loot Pickup:** Bouncing the Disk off at least 1 wall (or shattering glass/pots/leaves) builds a centrifugal vortex that snatches dropped items along its flight path and delivers them directly into your inventory upon return[cite: 1, 2].
* **Ricochet Skill-Shot Bonus:** Every wall bounce adds **+1.5 bonus damage** to the throw[cite: 1, 2].

## Blueprint Forge Upgrades

Sneak + Right-Click an **Anvil** (Mineclonia) or **Steel Block / Furnace** (Minetest Game) while holding your Disk to customize three modular layers[cite: 1, 2]:

### 1. Outer Shell (Flight Physics)
* **Chakram Ring (`1x Iron/Steel`):** *Agile* — +2 Max Wall Bounces and 20% faster flight speed[cite: 1, 2].
* **Round Shield (`3x Iron/Steel`):** *Heavy Shield* — Enables `Sneak + Left-Click` Shield Bash, doubles projectile parry radius, and deals double knockback[cite: 1, 2].
* **Sawtooth Blade (`2x Iron/Steel`):** *Grinder* — Deals +2 Bleed damage on contact, losing 15% flight speed when slicing through flesh[cite: 1, 2].
* **Shuriken Star (`2x Iron/Steel`):** *Piercer* — Slices through endless lines of enemies with zero momentum loss[cite: 1, 2].

### 2. Core Module (Special Abilities)
* **Hollow Ring (`Free`):** *Lightweight* — Minimal air drag so the Disk flies much farther before losing momentum[cite: 1, 2].
* **Iron Cross (`4x Iron/Steel`):** *Smasher* — Hitting a solid wall triggers a 3x3 AoE shockwave, dealing splash damage to nearby mobs[cite: 1, 2].
* **Star Emblem (`2x Gold Ingot`):** *Loyal* — Automatically pulls itself free after sticking into a surface and returns to your hand, damaging enemies on the return flight[cite: 1, 2].
* **Energy Core (`3x Redstone / Copper`):** *Recall & Magnet* — Unlocks manual `Right-Click` recall from anywhere, triples the Momentum Loot Pickup radius (5 blocks), and glows in the dark[cite: 1, 2].

### 3. Structural Alloy (Dyes)
Tint your blade and impact sparks using **Polished Steel** (Free), **Crimson Red**, **Cobalt Blue**, **Royal Gold**, **Emerald Green**, or **Obsidian Black** (`1x Dye` each)[cite: 1, 2].

## Mineclonia Enchanting Support

In Mineclonia, the Disk can be enchanted at an **Enchanting Table** or **Anvil**[cite: 1, 2]:
* **Loyalty I–III:** Automatically returns the Disk to your hand after it sticks into a surface (faster return per level)[cite: 3].
* **Sharpness I–V:** Increases both outward and return-strike blade damage[cite: 1, 2].
* **Impaling I–V:** Adds bonus piercing damage[cite: 3].
* **Unbreaking I–III:** Greatly reduces durability wear from throwing and combat hits[cite: 1, 2].
* **Knockback / Punch:** Increases impact knockback against mobs[cite: 1, 2].
